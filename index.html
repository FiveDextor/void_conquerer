COMBAT

World War battlefields. Command your squad and hold the line.

Tap one of your units, tap a highlighted tile to move. Each unit moves once and acts once per turn. Tap a tile to walk there by the shortest path, or press and drag from a unit to draw its own path; the arrow stays until you click it to confirm (click elsewhere to cancel). Units attack only with their Skill: press Skill, then tap a red square (the percent shows how much damage lands). Tap your barracks to spawn a unit within 1 tile, paid with money; you earn $10 per turn plus $5 per flag you hold. Infantry can capture enemy outposts next to them, which uses that unit's movement for the turn. Outposts heal nearby allies with Supply Cargo. Right-click and drag (or drag empty ground) to pan big maps. Press End Turn when done. Win by capturing or destroying every enemy outpost, or by wiping out the enemy. Fog of war hides enemy units until one of your units sees them (sight is its range plus 2), but structures are always visible. Units in forests are only spotted from 1 tile away. Hills and bunkers are infantry only, and a unit on high ground can shoot down but never up.
