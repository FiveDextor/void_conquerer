COMBAT

World War battlefields. Command your squad and hold the line.

Tap one of your units, tap a highlighted tile to move. Each unit moves once and acts once per turn. Tap a tile to walk there by the shortest path, or press and drag from a unit to draw its own path; the arrow stays until you click it to confirm (click elsewhere to cancel). Units attack only with their Skill: press Skill, then tap a red square (the number shows predicted damage). Tap your barracks to spawn a unit on a neighboring tile, paid with money; you earn $10 per turn plus $5 per outpost you hold. Select an infantry unit to outline an adjacent enemy outpost in yellow, then click the outpost to capture it. Capturing changes ownership but does not move the unit, and uses that unit's movement for the turn. Outposts heal nearby allies with Supply Cargo. Right-click and drag (or drag empty ground) to pan big maps. Press End Turn when done. Capture or destroy every enemy outpost to win; destroying enemy units alone is not enough. Fog of war hides enemy units until one of your units sees them (sight is its range plus 2), but structures are always visible. Units in forests are only spotted from 1 tile away. Hills and bunkers are infantry only, and a unit on high ground can shoot down but never up.
